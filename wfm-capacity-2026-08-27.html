Hour-by-Hour Capacity Detail — Industry + Revenue
How to read the On-Target Closures columns 1.7 is the daily closure target per engineer — each engineer is expected to close 1.7 cases by the end of their 9-hour shift. The columns show how many cases each shift should have closed by this IST hour if they are running on target.

Formula: HC × 1.7 × (hours elapsed ÷ 9)
Example: APAC (21 HC) starts at 5:30 AM. Full-day target = 21 × 1.7 = 35.7 cases. By 9:30 AM (4 hrs into a 9-hr shift) → 21 × 1.7 × (4÷9) = 15.9 cases should be closed by now if on target. By end of shift at 2:30 PM = 35.7 (full target met).

Cases/Day is the inflow signal — the actual demand arriving each hour. This is what drives queue growth.

On-Target vs Inflow = total on-target closures across all active shifts minus Cases/Day arriving that hour.
Green / positive = shifts are closing more than the inflow arriving — queue is being absorbed.
Red / negative = inflow exceeds on-target closures — cases are building in the queue. A shift showing — has not started yet at that hour.

Example at 9:30 AM IST — two shifts active:
  · APAC (21 HC, 4 hrs in): 21 × 1.7 × (4÷9) = 15.9 on-target closures
  · India (43 HC, 1 hr in): 43 × 1.7 × (1÷9) = 8.1 on-target closures
  · Total on-target = 24.0  |  Cases/Day (inflow) at 9:30 AM = 5.1
  · On-Target vs Inflow = 24.0 − 5.1 = +18.9 — shifts are closing faster than inflow at this hour.
Compare to 8:30 PM where 100.2 cases/day arrive — that is where the red appears and queue builds.
