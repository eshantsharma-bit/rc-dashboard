Staffing Recommendations
🔴 Critical — Peak Coverage Gap (7:30–8:30 PM IST)
Hours 19–20 carry 37.8% of all daily Industry cases (~163 cases/day) but only 13.1% of staffing. This maps to 7–9 AM PDT — the US morning ramp.

Recommended fix: Extend EMEA shift end from 9:30 PM to 10:30 PM (+1 hr, +47 HC) and pull forward PST start from 8:30 PM to 7:30 PM (+1 hr, +46 HC). Combined adds ~93 HC to the most critical hours at zero new headcount.
🔴 High — Midnight Gap (12:30 AM IST)
Hour 0 (12:30 AM IST = 12 PM PDT) carries 7.8% of daily demand (~36.8 cases/day) but only 4.9% of staffing (92 HC). This is US noon — a natural inflow spike as East Coast engineers open cases after lunch.

Recommended fix: EST shift currently runs 5:30 PM–2:30 AM IST. Extend by 1 hour to 12:30 AM → 2:30 AM gives EST coverage through midnight. Alternatively, a small IST late-night cohort (5 engineers) can absorb the gap.
🟡 Optimise — APAC/India Overlap (9:30 AM–2:30 PM IST)
Hours 9–13 have both APAC (21 HC) and India (43 HC) active simultaneously — 64–110 engineers covering only 1.1–3.4% of daily demand each hour. This is US overnight / early morning (9 PM–2 AM PDT) when customers are not active.

Recommended fix: Reduce APAC headcount in hours 12–13 (wind down earlier, e.g., 1:30 PM instead of 2:30 PM). Redeploy 10–14 APAC engineers to a late-evening IST window (6–8 PM IST) where demand surges.
🟡 Optimise — India Shift End Alignment
India engineers (43 HC) wind down at 6:30 PM IST. At 7:30 PM (hr19), India HC drops to 0 just as Industry volume spikes from ~14 to 70 cases/day. India shift ending at 6:30 PM IST = 6 AM PDT, before the US morning surge even begins.

Recommended fix: Introduce a late IST cohort (e.g., 12:30 PM – 9:30 PM IST) for 15–20 India engineers, creating overlap with the peak hrs 19–20 window. Existing India engineers on standard shift keep 9:30 AM–6:30 PM.
🟢 Efficiency — Revenue Cloud Understaffed vs Industry
Revenue Cloud carries 22.4% of case volume but only has 26.2% of HC (489 of 1,867). Revenue's peak is more evenly distributed (max 7.9% at hr19) vs Industry's extreme spike (18.6% at hr20).

Recommended fix: Revenue staffing is structurally reasonable. No headcount add needed — focus should be on ensuring Revenue engineers at hrs 19–20 (29–38 HC) are not pulled to admin tasks during the peak window.
🟢 Baseline — EST/PST Night Coverage Is Adequate
Hours 1–4 (1:30–4:30 AM IST = 1–4 PM PDT, US afternoon) have adequate coverage: 34–92 HC covering 0.9–2.6% demand each. PST (46 HC) + EST residual provides solid overnight IST coverage.

No action needed for these hours. This is the best-matched block in the 24-hour cycle. Maintain current EST/PST staffing levels for this window.
