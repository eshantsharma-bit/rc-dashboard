The Story 5-week narrative · Jun 24 → Jul 30
Aged % jumped and stayed elevated. On Jun 24, org aged % was 17.2% — healthy. By Jul 2 it spiked to 22.1% (+4.9pp in one week) and has not recovered. Over the 5 weeks since, it has oscillated between 21.3% and 23.1%, with Jul 30 at 22.7%. This is not a transient blip — it represents a structural shift. The backlog is bigger and harder to clear, and cases are stacking up faster than teams can resolve them.
Inflow is rising, compressing resolution capacity. June averaged ~693 cases/week across all products. The week of Jul 19–25 hit 779 cases (+12.4% vs Jun avg) — the highest in at least 6 weeks. Revenue Cloud drove an 18% WoW inflow surge in that period (301 cases, led by Configurator +25). Rising inflow with a stable team size means each engineer carries more cases, directly driving aged % up.
Retail & CG: Structurally stuck, not a capacity issue. Aged % has been between 27–42% for all 5 weeks. The BL is flat at 72–80 cases, meaning engineers are working the cases but not resolving them. Root cause: Mondelēz (3 open aged cases) and Bacardi (3 cases) have complex customisation dependencies with Product Management. Until PM engages, these cases won't close. This is an account-escalation problem, not an engineer-capacity problem.
CPQ/OM/DC: Engineers not formally logging GUS investigations is the hidden blocker. Aged % is persistently 24–30% over 5 weeks. On Jul 30, 29 of 33 aged cases have no GUS investigation number logged. Engineers are working cases without a formal product defect path visible. BT Group (16-case cluster) and Sky Italia (45-day P1) are the two highest-risk accounts. Without engineers logging a GUS investigation through the formal process, there is no product team accountability and cases continue to age.
Omnistudio: The one P&T improving. Peaked at 25.4% (Jul 16), now at 18.5% — 5-week low. BL is growing slightly (+81) but aged count falling (15). Saheb's team is executing well, with 21 swarms across 15 engineers and strong closure rates. The trajectory is positive.
Life Sciences: Volume pressure building. Largest aged pool (40 cases) with BL growing (185 → 208 range). IST carries 88% of load — Bhagat and Naren's teams are the primary absorbers. Takeda (28 cases) + Deloitte (29 cases) account for 37% of total volume. iPad parity gaps are the dominant product signal (32% of EMEA cases, top engineering topic).
Revenue Cloud: Held the line despite surge. Strong execution during Jul 19–25 SHIFTLOGIC partner event (6×S1, go-live risk). Aged % hit a low of 13.5% on Jul 24 — best in 5 weeks. The team closed faster than cases aged. Sub-P&T watch: CPQ Dev Support at 27.8% on a small backlog (18 cases) — skill gap signal.
