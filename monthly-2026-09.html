💡 Key Insights — Jun–Sep 2026

📈 Inflow: Release-Driven Surges on a Rising Baseline

Both Jun (1,533) and Sep (1,410) were elevated by release cycle activity — higher post-release case volume is a recurring pattern as customers upgrade and hit new issues. Jul–Aug moderated as the release stabilised. Stripping release and outage effects, the underlying RCA adoption curve is consistently rising — the team should treat 1,400+ per month as the new normal, not a spike. Oct is on course to match or exceed Jun levels, making capacity planning a now conversation.

🗂 RCA Acceleration is the Structural Shift

RCA grew from 61% → 72% share in 4 months — and +56% in absolute volume. Legacy CPQ is flat to declining (end-of-life trajectory). India carries the highest RCA concentration (77%) — India engineers are at or near RCA saturation while CPQ capacity sits unused. Allocation review needed for Oct planning.

✅ Quality: SLO + Reopen Improving

Zero SLO misses in Aug and Sep — strongest consecutive run in the window. Reopen Rate trending from 7.0% → 6.0% (−1pp in 4 months). TTR improved in Aug (4.8d best in window) and held in Sep (5.2d). Operational quality is healthy despite volume pressure — the team absorbed the Sep surge without a quality regression.

🐛 Bug Rate Spike Needs Watching

Bug Rate surged from 3.5% (Aug) to 8.2% (Sep) — the largest single-month jump in the window. Investigation Rate also rising (4.2% → 8.8%), indicating engineering engagement is up. Both signals are consistent with R264 GA defect discovery as customer adoption of 264 widens. Expect further elevation in Oct as 264 GA penetration grows.

⭐ CSAT: Sep Dip — 5 Low-Score Responses

Sep CSAT 4.71 is the first dip in 4 months (Aug was 4.84, the period high). The dip correlates with the elevated S1 volume in Sep — outage-heavy weeks tend to suppress scores even on well-handled cases. 5 low-score responses in Sep vs 0–2 in prior months. Key accounts: William Reed (2/5, RCA) and Just Energy (1/5, Billing). Team remains well above industry benchmarks in absolute terms.
