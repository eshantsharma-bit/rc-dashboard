Hourly Demand % (Chat) vs Staffing % — IST

Understaffed — 7:30–9:30 PM IST (Hours 19–21)

The chat evening peak runs 7:30–9:30 PM IST (7–9 AM PDT) carrying 21% of daily chat demand (~286 cases/day) against only ~17.5% of staffing. Unlike all-origins where hours 19–20 spike to 37%, chat has a broader, shallower peak — manageable but still structurally understaffed by 3–4pp each hour.

Late Night IST Gap — Hrs 5–8 (5:30–8:30 AM IST)

Hours 5–8 (5–9 PM PDT, US afternoon) carry 6.8% of chat demand (~93 cases/day) but only 4.5% of staffing (84 HC). This is the US West Coast afternoon window — customers are active on chat, but IST headcount is at its lowest. Gap of ~−0.6pp per hour consistently across these 4 hours.

Well-matched — 12:30 PM–5:30 PM IST (Hours 12–17)

IST afternoon hours carry 29% of chat demand and have 34.8% of staffing — a modest surplus. These hours map to US overnight/early morning (12 AM–6 AM PDT) when case volume is naturally lower. Chat is better distributed here than all-origins, making the IST afternoon staffing a reasonable coverage band.

Key Difference vs All-Origins

Chat demand is far more evenly spread across the day. The all-origins model has a structural crisis at 8:30 PM IST (20.7% of volume); chat caps at 7.5%. This means chat-focused teams are better served by current shifts than all-origin teams. The priority fix is the 7:30–9:30 PM IST block, not a wholesale shift restructure.
