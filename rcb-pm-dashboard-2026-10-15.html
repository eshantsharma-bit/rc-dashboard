Executive Summary

What the data says

  • 57% of cases are "User Needs Education" — customers can't find how to do something. This is a documentation and onboarding gap, not a product defect.
  • 10% are confirmed product bugs (85 cases) — but 37 unique bugs filed across 886 cases means many cases share the same underlying defect. Fix 1 bug = deflect 5–10 cases.
  • TTR is healthy — RCB at 8.79d, RCA at 7.46d, both well within 10d target. Support team is resolving on time.
  • Billing Batch Scheduler named by 4 independent accounts in Summer '26 — a named regression, not isolated config issues.
  • Invoice generation is the #1 pain point (105 cases) — non-actionable error messages drive repeated case filing; customers can't self-recover.

PM action items from support signal

  • Error messages on invoice/billing failures — field-level, actionable errors would deflect 50+ cases/quarter (currently generic system errors).
  • Stuck invoice recovery runbook — all stuck invoice cases escalate to engineering; a self-service playbook breaks the dependency.
  • W-23554160 (Payment field visibility) — ECi go-live blocker, 3 accounts affected, no ETA. Needs priority.
  • W-24088655 (State/Country picklist 264) — 3 accounts, invoice generation failing. 264 regression.
  • Tax: Avalara adapter + OOTB — EMEA/APAC/JP concentrated, 74 cases, multi-account cross-region pain.
  • Dunning/CollectionPlan extensibility — W-23968484 triaged but unscheduled; WebPT ramp shows growing need.
