⚠ Invoice Generation — Two Systemic Gaps (105 cases)

1 · Error Messages Are Not Actionable
Customers and support engineers see generic system-level errors — System.UnexpectedException: Missing data, ARM Invoice Generation Error, TaxCalculationFailed — with no indication of which record failed, which field is wrong, or what to fix. Cases for the same underlying root cause arrive worded differently from every customer because each one is guessing from the error surface they see. Support spends first-response time just decoding what actually failed.
2 · Invoices Stuck in Intermediate State — Support Has No Recovery Path
When an invoice enters a partial or stuck state (pending generation, generation initiated but not completed, batch partially run), support has no documented recovery playbook. There is no "how to recover a stuck invoice" KB article. Every such case becomes an engineering escalation — support cannot unblock the customer independently. This is a repeating pattern: the same escalation loop is occurring across different accounts and different engineers, with no institutional resolution.
Product signal: This is a tooling + documentation gap, not a one-off bug. Actionable error messages reduce escalation volume and enable customer self-service — customers can act on a clear message without opening a case. Recovery documentation breaks the engineering dependency — support resolves stuck invoices without an engineering assist every time. Both are high-leverage, low-code investments.

What the Data Shows
📈 Volume
• 886 cases · avg 110/mo · never below 88 in any month
• Jul dip (88) recovered fully — seasonal, not structural
• Sep partial (104 through Sep 24) on pace to exceed Apr peak (128)
• Partner Premier accounts (31%) drive consistent volume — multi-customer implementation partners filing on behalf of several end-orgs in a single case
• New account onboarding wave: 97 first-time filers in Feb–Mar alone
🐞 Defects
• 37 confirmed bugs filed; 4 span multiple independent accounts — platform-level defects, not isolated config issues
• 8 WAD investigations generating repeat cases — same product decision hitting different customers repeatedly
• 8 Feature Requests arriving via support — missing capability signals
• Bluvium Inc: Partner Premier running implementations for multiple end-customers — 15 Sev-1s across their portfolio, zero GUS filed; volume reflects their customer base breadth, not a single failing org
• Summer '26 Billing Batch Scheduler regression: 4 independent accounts named it explicitly in subject text
🔎 Patterns
• Billing Batch Scheduler: #1 named technical object across 57 cases — scheduler reliability is a systemic theme
• Tax failures EMEA/locale-concentrated — not a global product issue
• Evergreen billing after cancellation: 17 cases across different customers — customers being charged post-cancel
• Invoice Generation failures (105 cases) are the single largest category — batch errors, quota limits, generation not triggering
• Winter '27 signals appearing in Sep data (Catch-Up Bill Run, custom field issue)
