Key Observations for MBR
1. Industry-Life Sciences: 4-month unchecked climb (+74% from April). At 607 cases (18.1% of total), 10.9% bug rate, 53.2% of all bugs, and 51.2% of all investigations — this product needs a cross-functional review before August. Takeda (103), Deloitte (66), and Pfizer (64) are the anchor accounts.
2. India TTR back above 10 days (10.39) — an owner bandwidth and case complexity issue. India carries 200 open S1/S2 cases. Rajath Nagaraj (9 cases, avg 16.3 days) and Abhishek Dixit (8 cases, avg 18.2 days) are the highest-load owners. Takeda (21 open) and Deloitte (17 open) account clusters are driving elongated resolution cycles. Meanwhile EMEA TTR improved to 6.94 — a 3-month consecutive improvement.
3. Deloitte group (72 + 44 = 116 cases, 17 S1) — largest single-group risk. Deloitte Global Services S1 cases tripled over 3 months (4→10→17). Combined with Deloitte Consulting LLP, the group represents 3.5% of all July volume. This pattern indicates a platform or implementation-level issue, not isolated incidents.
4. Four new Sev1 account entrants in July. McDonald's (7 S1), N28 Technologies (6 S1), London Business School (5 S1), TATA Motors (5 S1) — all zero S1 in May and June. Four simultaneous first-time entrants suggests a common trigger, possibly a recent release or migration.
5. Sev1 improved MoM (-9.3%, 367→333), led by India recovery. India S1 fell from 217→184 (-15.3%), returning toward May levels. EMEA S1 has been structurally flat at ~149 for 3 months — no improvement trend. The overall improvement is India-driven.
6. Positive signals: EMEA TTR, Closures, Throughput, Escalation %. EMEA TTR at 6.94 (3-month best). Closures at 3,485 (highest in 3 months). Throughput at 1.93 (steady improvement). Escalation % dropped to 0.5% from 0.7%. EMEA operational engine is healthy.
7. Chat Mix steady climb: 14.7% → 15.8% → 16.6%. Three consecutive monthly increases. At current pace (~0.9pp/month), the 30% target is an 18-month horizon — worth discussing if there's a plan to accelerate adoption.
8. Takeda volume doubled MoM (73→144). Predominantly S2 via India (102 of 144 cases). Not yet S1-heavy but the volume acceleration warrants proactive outreach. Specifically 13 open S2 cases still in "Working" or "Need More Info" status.
