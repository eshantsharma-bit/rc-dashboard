Key Findings — What changes when Chat is excluded?

  • Chat inflates <3d % significantly: Removing Chat drops overall <3d rate by ~8–11pp each month. Chat cases are simpler/faster to close — their presence flatters the metric.
  • EMEA most inflated: EMEA drops ~10–18pp without Chat (e.g. Oct: 48.2% → 29.9%). Chat handles a large share of EMEA's quick closes.
  • APAC most inflated: APAC drops ~12–20pp (Oct: 40.9% → 21.6%). Without Chat, APAC <3d rate is the lowest of all regions.
  • India least affected: India changes only ±0–3pp — India's <3d rate is genuine engineer throughput, not Chat-driven.
  • Legacy CPQ most chat-dependent: Legacy CPQ drops ~14–20pp without Chat — the highest delta of any P&T. Its apparent strong performance is largely Chat-driven.
  • Nov 26 trend: Nov overall (excl. Chat) is at the lowest point in 5 months at 23.4% — signals a genuine resolution slowdown.
