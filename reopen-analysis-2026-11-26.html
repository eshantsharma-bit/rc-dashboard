TTR Impact — Never-Reopened vs Reopened · By Region
TTR = Age_days__c (creation → final close). Re TTR = avg for cases with Reopen_count__c > 0. NR TTR = avg for Reopen_count__c = 0 (2,000-record sample per region).

Multiplier = Re TTR ÷ NR TTR. A 2.15× org multiplier means every reopened case consumes 2× the engineer capacity and delivers 2× worse customer experience. EMEA has the best multiplier (1.83×) — lower reopen volume and faster NR baseline.
