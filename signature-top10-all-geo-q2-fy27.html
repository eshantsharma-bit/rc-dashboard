Account Cluster Analysis
Three distinct groups in the top 10
🧬
Life Sciences Cluster (4 accounts · 591 cases · 59%)
Pfizer, Deloitte, Mylan, Apsen. All Q2-heavy with new/expanding deployments. Two are brand new (Mylan +7,200%, Apsen +983%). Deloitte is the SI driving LSC implementations globally — its growth (+42%) tracks the pharma customer base, not a single account.
📦
Telecom / CPQ Cluster (4 accounts · 274 cases · 27%)
BT Digico, Claro, BT Consumer, One New Zealand. All on CPQ/Order Management or Comm Cloud. 3 of 4 declining or stable QoQ — deployment maturing. BT Consumer (+45%) is the exception, on EPC ramp.
⚡
Revenue Cloud Cluster (2 accounts · 127 cases · 13%)
Bell Canada (+39%) and Equinix (-42%) move in opposite directions. Bell scaling up on DRO+Configurator+TxnMgmt. Equinix improving significantly. Both near 100% Revenue Cloud — concentrated RC complexity warranting RC product-team alignment.

Executive Insights — Key Signals for QBR
Risk Signals — Needs Attention
Accounts or patterns requiring active CSM or product engagement
⚠️
Mylan + Apsen: New Deployments Need Dedicated Coverage
Two pharma accounts went from near-zero to combined 139 cases in Q2. Both are 100% Life Sciences. Mylan spans 3 GEOs. Without deliberate onboarding support and early-life engineering alignment, these accounts risk becoming Pfizer-scale volume spikes in Q3.
📈
Bell Canada: RC Volume Rising Fast in AMER
+39% QoQ growth, all Revenue Cloud (DRO + Configurator + Transaction Mgmt). Only 64 cases in Q2 but on a steep trajectory — was just 3 cases in Q2 FY26. RC specialist coverage must keep pace. Telecom RC implementations typically plateau after 2–3 quarters; we're in the steep part of the curve.
🌏
India Absorbing Majority of Cross-GEO Load
India is the #1 handling region for 7 of top 10 accounts — including EMEA-named accounts (Pfizer, BT Digico, Claro) and APAC accounts (One NZ). IST Life Sciences team capacity needs to scale with the new pharma deployment wave (Mylan, Apsen, Deloitte).
Positive Signals — Strong Performance
Accounts and trends showing healthy patterns
↘
Pfizer Stabilising — Largest Account Declining
Pfizer dropped from 448 → 343 (-23% QoQ). Still #1 globally but the trend is in the right direction as the initial Life Sciences deployment matures. If Q3 continues this trajectory, Pfizer will drop out of the top 5 — a strong portfolio health signal.
✓
Equinix: Major Improvement (-42%)
Was #3 in Q1 with 108 cases, down to 63 in Q2 (-42%). Near-100% Revenue Cloud. Strong resolution progress. Recommend CSM verification that this is genuine stabilisation vs. deferred backlog — but the initial signal is very positive.
📉
Dropout Accounts All Improving
3 Q1 top-10 accounts (Fennia, Telecom Argentina, AT&T) all fell out due to volume reduction — not because new accounts were added. All show 30–66% QoQ declines. Combined they freed ~130 cases of capacity headroom in the top-10 bracket.
