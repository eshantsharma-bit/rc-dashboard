🚨 IR case volume scales with org growth — expected, not alarming As the RC installed base grew from 765 → 1,908 orgs (+149%), IR cases on the first 7 days have stayed between 12–60. 264's 43 IR cases on 1,908 orgs reflects an IR-to-org ratio of 2.3% — the second-lowest ever. The absolute count trending down (60 → 51 → 12 → 43) is the right direction.
⚡ 264 introduces two new IR topics not seen in any prior upgrade Billing & Invoicing (14% of IR cases) and Usage & Ratings (11%) appear for the first time across upgrade windows. These areas had zero IR cases in 258, 260, and 262 combined. Both are tied to specific bugs already in fix pipeline (W-24088655 closed/264.e, W-23791512 closed/266).
✅ Configurator share in IR cases declining every release In 258, 50% of IR cases were Configurator (PlaceSalesTransaction failures, CML errors). By 264, that dropped to 34% — showing each release is resolving the most common Configurator regressions more proactively. Sev1 IR also improved: 23 (258) → 11 (260) → 6 (262) → 7 (264).
📊 264 at a glance: 43 IR cases · 13 bugs · 35 cases with a bug attached Of 43 IR cases, 29 have a bug ID attached — a 67% bug-coverage rate. 13 unique bugs identified in first 7 days. Top bug by case volume: W-24059609 (6 cases) — cross-context field mapping dropped post-264 upgrade; already fixed in 264.e.
🔍 Product Catalog Management emerges as new watch area in 264 W-24096152 — email sanitisation breaking product details API — drove 5 PCM cases (search index rebuild failures). Classified as Configurator in the case tag but the root cause is PCM. Bug closed in 264.e, but accounts like Genesys, Vestas, and TAPCO were blocked on product search.
