Reopen Deep-Dive — Classification, Auto-Close & TTR Impact · All 4 Regions · May–Oct 2026

41,340 cases opened May–Oct 2026 across AMER · EMEA · India · APAC.  Of these, 2,845 (6.9%) were reopened at least once. Every reopened case takes 2.15× longer to finally close — burning ~915 engineer-months in rework. Separately, 1,725 cases (4.2%) were auto-closed after 3 unresponsive follow-ups — these are customers who went silent and are a leading indicator of potential churn or unresolved issues.

HOW TO BUILD IT — AI Close Gate (Hackathon Proposal)
The Problem
82.8% of reopens come from cases already marked "Solution Provided" — the engineer believed it was done, but the customer disagreed.

These are not random. They cluster around specific products (RCA, Legacy CPQ), short close times on complex topics, and accounts with prior reopen history.

The pattern is detectable before the case is closed.
The Mechanism
Step 1 — Score at close time. When engineer moves to Solution Provided, an Agentforce action triggers and scores "reopen risk" using: product topic, case age at close, account reopen history, case subject similarity to known reopen patterns.

Step 2 — Friction for high-risk cases. If score > threshold: surface a prompt — "3 similar cases on this account were reopened. Confirm resolution with customer before proceeding?"

Step 3 — Track outcome. Cases that cleared the gate and still got reopened improve the model over time.
