Hourly Demand % vs Staffing % — IST

Critical Understaffing — 7:30–8:30 PM IST (Hours 19–20)

These two hours contain 36.9% of all daily cases (~7,800 in 60 days) but receive only 13.1% of available staffing — a combined deficit of 23.8pp. This window is 7–9 AM PDT, the US Pacific morning ramp when engineers and customers open cases as they start work. The structural mismatch is the single largest staffing risk.

Secondary Gap — 12:30 AM IST (Hour 00 / 12 PM PDT)

Midnight IST (US lunch hour on the Pacific coast) carries 8.1% of demand with only 4.9% staffing. Combined with hours 19–20, three hours of the day account for 45% of all demand but only 18% of staffing. Any SLA pressure will concentrate in these windows.

Overstaffed — 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM IST (9 hours)

IST business hours (9:30 AM–6:30 PM) carry ~32% of demand but ~51% of staffing — a 19pp surplus. Worst single hour: 5:30 PM IST (+3.2pp: 6.9% staff vs 3.7% demand). These are IST-convenient hours, but the actual case load is low during this window as most US customers haven't started their day yet.

Recommendation — Shift the Evening Cohort

Extending coverage into 6–9 PM IST (5–8 AM PDT) by shifting 3–4pp of staffing from the 12–18 IST block would directly address the peak. A dedicated late-evening IST cohort (e.g. 6 PM–2 AM IST) covering the 7–10 PM IST window could absorb 40%+ of daily volume without adding headcount.
